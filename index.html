Quick start: how to play in 30 seconds

  1. Press How to play: Training on the main menu. It walks you through every control with live prompts.
  2. Your goal is the BUILD bar. It fills while nodes are connected to the Core through clean links. Drag from node to node to connect them.
  3. Orange nodes are bugs. Click them to patch before their ring closes, or they scramble and cut their links.
  4. Watch CORRUPTION. If it hits 100% or the clock runs out, you lose.
  5. Click yellow CODE packets to take a Code Breach question. Right answers purge corruption and charge Override.
  6. When Override is full, press Space. Then pick Campaign or Quick match and play on.

One goal, one enemy, one deadline

ONE GOAL
Ship the build. Fill BUILD to 100% by keeping as many nodes as possible linked to the Core.
ONE ENEMY
The Sys-Overlord, a rogue AI. It never touches you. It injects bugs, spreads them and scrambles your links.
ONE DEADLINE
The match clock. Run out of time, or let CORRUPTION reach 100%, and the build is lost.

Controls

  • Drag from one node to another to link them. A ring shows your reach. Nodes hold 4 links, the Core holds 6. Drag across an existing link to cut it.
  • Only nodes connected to the Core through clean nodes count toward BUILD.
  • Click a bugged node to patch it before its ring closes. Chain patches within 4 seconds for a combo.
  • Shield tool (button, or S in Solo): click a healthy node to make it bug-proof for a while.
  • Override fills as you patch and as your network grows. Press Space for 8 seconds of triple build speed, free actions, frozen bugs and purged corruption.
  • Duo: Player 1 uses the mouse (Architect). Player 2 moves a reticle with WASD, patches or collects with E, shields with Q. Bandwidth is shared.
  • P pauses, M toggles sound.

Threat guide

  • !Bug: the standard threat. Spreads slowly, scrambles its node when the ring closes.
  • WWorm: spreads twice as often and more reliably. Contain it fast.
  • BLogic bomb: short fuse. If it explodes it kills the node and infects every neighbor. Patch it first.

Code Breach: coding questions in the match

  • A yellow CODE packet appears on your network. Click it to freeze the match and answer a question from DSA, CLI, LLM or advanced Python.
  • Right answer: +40 Override, -12 corruption, +20 bandwidth. Wrong answer or timeout: +8 corruption and two bugs.
  • Ignore a packet and it fades, injecting a bug into its node. Questions get tougher with level number and difficulty. Press 1 to 4 to answer fast, or type the command when asked.
  • Practice any time in the Code Lab from the main menu.

Mutators (campaign)

  • From level 21 each level carries mutators: Rate Limit (links cost double), Fragile Ports, Drought, Echo Offline, Zero-Day Storm, Cascade, Bomber and Brittle Code. From level 121 you get two, from level 301 three.
  • Every 25th level is a sector boss. Each sector has its own color theme and music key.

Pickups and boss attacks

  • Data packets appear on your network and fade in 9 seconds. Click one to collect: +35 bandwidth, +25 override or +8 seconds on the deadline.
  • From aggression LV3 on higher difficulties the Sys-Overlord uses Fork Bomb (three bugs at once), Shield Breaker (strips every shield) and Rootkit (turns a healthy node into a logic bomb).
  • Keep the Core's neighbors shielded, patch bombs before worms and worms before bugs, and save Override for a storm.
